GTA6 Clips

Personal content tool · Last updated November 19, 2026

GTA6 Clips is a personal tool used by its owner to edit highlights of public Grand Theft Auto VI live streams into short vertical videos and daily compilations, and to upload them to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

The tool runs only on the owner's computer. It is not offered to other users and does not collect data from the public.

How it uses YouTube and TikTok

  • YouTube Data API: uploads videos, sets thumbnails and posts a comment on the owner's own channel.
  • TikTok Content Posting API: publishes videos to the owner's own TikTok account.

Every clip credits the original streamer and links to their channel.
