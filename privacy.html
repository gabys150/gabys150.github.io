Privacy Policy

GTA6 Clips · Effective November 19, 2026

GTA6 Clips ("the app") is a personal tool operated by its owner. This policy explains what the app accesses and how.

1. Data the app accesses

  • Google / YouTube account (owner only): through Google OAuth, the app receives an access token that allows it to upload videos, set thumbnails and post comments on the owner's YouTube channel. It does not read private videos, subscribers, messages or any data of other users.
  • TikTok account (owner only): through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token and basic profile info (open ID, display name) needed to publish videos to the owner's account.
  • Public live streams: the app processes publicly broadcast Twitch, Kick and YouTube live streams to create edited highlights. It does not collect personal data about viewers.

2. How data is stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in local files. No data is sent to any server operated by the app, and no analytics, cookies or tracking are used.

3. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Videos and their metadata are sent only to YouTube and TikTok, at the owner's request, to be published.

4. Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. TikTok

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. TikTok's handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

6. Deletion

Revoking access (see above) and deleting the local token files removes all data held by the app.
